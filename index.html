Pressy support

Pressy is a blood pressure log for iPhone. It guides the 7-day home protocol and turns your readings into a report for your doctor. Your readings stay on your phone.

Contact

Email bplog.support@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone, in the app's own database. There is no account and no cloud copy. If you allow Apple Health, readings are also read from and written to Health on the same phone.

How do I share readings with my doctor? Open the Report tab. Share CSV is free. Export PDF is part of Pressy Pro.

How do I cancel Pressy Pro? Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Pressy. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends.

I changed phones. How do I restore Pro? In Pressy, open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete everything? In Pressy, open Settings and tap Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes its data. Readings already saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you remove them there.

Does Pressy measure my blood pressure? No. Pressy is a log, not a medical device. Use your own cuff and type the numbers in, or let Apple Health bring them in. If you have chest pain or trouble breathing, seek emergency care.
