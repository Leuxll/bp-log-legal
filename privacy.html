Pressy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-24
Who we are: Yue Fung Lee, the developer of Pressy ("we", "us").
Contact: bplog.support@gmail.com

Pressy is a blood pressure log. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your phone, and the choices you have. In short: your readings stay on your iPhone, there is no account, and we do not sell your data or show ads.

What stays on your phone

Pressy stores the following only on your iPhone, in the app's own database:

  • Blood pressure and pulse readings you type in, with the time, arm, position, tags and any note you add.
  • The details you enter for your report: your name, medications, conditions, your usual arm and your next appointment date.
  • The answers you give during setup, such as why you are tracking, the problems you pick and whether you chose to start now.
  • Readings imported from Apple Health, if you allow it.
  • Your settings, such as reminder times.

We cannot see this information. It is not uploaded to us or to any server we operate. If you delete the app, all of it is erased from your phone. Settings > Delete all data erases your readings, your report details and your settings. It does not remove four small things: the label of which setup flow you were shown, the random analytics identifier and the experiment settings the app has saved, and any trial-ending reminder already scheduled. The setup-flow label and the analytics identifier are removed when you delete the app. A scheduled trial reminder is removed when your subscription state changes or when you delete the app. Readings the app already saved to Apple Health are not removed by either; they stay in Apple Health until you delete them there. Your iPhone's own backup (iCloud or a computer) may keep a copy of the app's data as part of a normal device backup, under your Apple settings.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Pressy reads blood pressure and heart rate samples from Apple Health so readings from your cuff appear in the app, and saves the readings you type in to Apple Health. You control this in the iOS Health settings and can turn it off at any time. Health data is used only inside the app to show your readings and build your report. We do not send Apple Health data to us, to analytics, or to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising.

Reports you share

When you export a PDF or CSV report, the app creates the file on your phone and opens the iOS share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send it, such as your doctor, Files or Mail. Once you share it, the receiving app or person handles it under their own rules.

Information we collect

We collect a small amount of anonymous information to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number, and Pressy has no account.

Product analytics (PostHog). We use PostHog to record anonymous events such as "onboarding step viewed", "paywall viewed", "report exported" and "reading logged". A protocol start is counted only when you start a week from the Today screen, never when you finish setup, so the count does not reveal your setup answers. The app also records, automatically through PostHog, when it is installed, opened, sent to the background and brought back to the front. These events say that something happened. They never contain a blood pressure value, pulse, note, medication, name, appointment date or anything from Apple Health. Events are tied to a random identifier the app generates on your phone, not to you. We switch off IP-based location lookup. PostHog may also receive basic technical details that come with any app event, such as the app version, iOS version and device model. PostHog servers are operated by PostHog Inc. in the United States.

Experiments. The same service tells the app which version of a screen to show (for example, which wording is on the subscription screen) so we can compare versions. It uses the same random identifier, and we keep a record of which version the app was given. When the app reads an experiment setting, PostHog automatically sends a "feature flag called" event that names the setting and the version shown.

Crash reports. If the app hits an unexpected error, a crash report with the technical error and stack trace can be sent through the same service so we can fix it. We do not capture the app's console output or screen contents.

Purchases (RevenueCat and Apple). If you subscribe, Apple processes the payment and RevenueCat, our subscription provider, receives a purchase receipt and a random app user identifier to tell the app whether you have an active subscription. We never see your card details or Apple ID. RevenueCat's servers are located in the United States.

Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. They are local notifications. We do not send push notifications from a server.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not show advertising and we do not use data for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not use session replay, screenshots or keystroke capture.
  • We do not ask you to create an account.

Legal bases and your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe and the UK, PIPEDA in Canada, or state laws in the United States), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Because your readings never reach us and the analytics identifier is random and anonymous, we cannot look up or link any data to you. You can erase your readings, report details and settings with Settings > Delete all data (the exceptions are listed above), turn off Apple Health in iOS Settings, and delete the app. Because we cannot tell which anonymous analytics belong to you, we cannot look them up or delete them for you. The identifier is stored on your phone, and deleting the app removes it from your device. To ask us anything about this policy, write to bplog.support@gmail.com.

We process the anonymous information above on the basis of our legitimate interest in running and improving the app, and the purchase information to provide the subscription you ask for.

Where information goes

Anonymous analytics and crash reports and subscription information are processed by our providers in the United States and possibly other countries. By using the app you understand that this information may be transferred outside your country.

How long we keep it

Anonymous analytics events and crash reports are kept for as long as our analytics provider's retention settings allow, which we set to the shortest period that is useful for improving the app. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as needed to run subscriptions and meet legal requirements.

Children

Pressy is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Your readings are stored in your iPhone's protected app storage. Data sent to our providers travels over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Medical information

Pressy is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. See our Terms of Service.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
